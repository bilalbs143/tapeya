Final Tape Ball App Specification
=================================

Actors & Responsibilities
- **Admin**: Launches tournaments, verifies content, manages live streams, approves merchant listings, and oversees the entire platform experience (including drafts, ecommerce, rewards, and lucky draws). Admins can download/delete live streams, add products for verified sellers, configure spend-based incentives, and enforce tournament templates/checklists.
- **User**: Has full platform access, acting as player, team manager, scorer, and content creator. Users can create/update teams, register for tournaments, submit scorecards, capture reels (likes/comments), join drafts, initiate open matches, participate in ecommerce, and claim or track rewards and lucky draw eligibility. This role also doubles as an organizer when creating tournaments, scheduling matches, and managing draft flows.
- **Sponsor**: Selects players during drafting rounds (one pick per round, randomized order), reviews registered rosters, and helps define tournaments by approving player selections. Sponsors also gain analytics on player engagement and ROI and can participate in leaderboards/standings views.
- **Shopkeeper/Seller**: Contacts the platform to sign MOUs, lists sporting goods (bats, balls, protective gear), fulfills orders, and can reward app-download customers with counter discounts.

App Screens (Player/Organizer Experience)
---------------------------------------
1. **Dashboard**
   - Status badges for tournaments (registration, drafting, live) with next-step CTAs, quick summaries of upcoming matches, outstanding draft picks, ecommerce promotions, notifications from live streams/lucky draws/reels, and spend-progress toward incentives.
2. **Tournament Creation**
   - Form for name, format, venues, prizes, registration/draft windows, sponsor assignments, and templates. Shows a checklist for registration, drafting, and scheduling tasks and exposes reminders for sponsors and live-stream ops once the tournament is published.
3. **Player Registration**
   - Countdown banner for the registration window, role-specific sign-up (batsman, bowler, all-rounder, wicketkeeper), metadata capture (batting hand, bowling style), downloads grouped by role, and a confirmed roster that feeds drafting/match eligibility.
4. **Drafting Suite**
   - Sponsor draft board with shuffled pick order, filtered registered pool, 14–15 player caps per team, live queue of locked-in players, and manual override controls. User notifications highlight sponsor turns and dice outcomes that determine the next picker.
5. **Match Scheduling**
   - Auto-generated group schedules, manual override controls (dates, venues, marquee fixtures), and synced reminders for teams/scorers/live streams. Links to scorecards, reels, and standings update when schedules change.
6. **Match Room**
   - Toss interface triggering scorecard creation, playing-eleven confirmation, ball-by-ball scoring that feeds stats engine (runs, wickets, strike rates, economy), automatic Man of the Match with manual override, live-stream toggles, and reel capture buttons.
7. **Standings & Stats**
   - Leaderboard with rankings, points, net run rate, auto/manual Man of the Match, player highlights, historical exports, and a stats timeline fed by each ball logged. Displays how stats impact drafts, draws, and ecommerce rewards.
8. **Reels Experience (Standalone)**
   - Creator studio for uploading clips, templates/filters, captions, tournament tags, share buttons (WhatsApp/Instagram), and engagement feed with likes/comments. Basic analytics available to all creators; premium tiers unlock deeper insights.
9. **Subscriptions & Access**
   - Tier selector summarizing Sponsor Teams, Reels Creators, Reels + Teams, and Premium bundles. Shows benefits, upgrade CTAs, and usage counters (reel limits, team quotas).
10. **Ecommerce Marketplace**
    - Seller onboarding widget (contact form/support chat), admin product dashboard (inventory, promos), public storefront (products, reviews, loyalty/app-download offers), order tracker linking to payout history, and loyalty incentives.
11. **Lucky Draw & Incentives**
    - Spend tracker toward threshold (e.g., Rs. 10k), qualification modal when met, prize pool (dinner, cash, vouchers), draw event page with winners, upcoming dates, and fulfillment statuses. Supports secondary rewards for participants who don’t win.
12. **Tape Ball Game**
    - Virtual tournament builder with rule enforcement, match entry lobby (scheduled fixtures/open matches), real-time tracking (scorecards, live streams, reels), and reward dashboard (participation points, standings, draw entries, ecommerce vouchers).

Admin Screens
-------------
- **Tournament Control Center**: Lists tournaments with status badges, lets admins edit templates, confirm sponsor picks, publish registration/draft windows, and manage notifications.
- **Draft Oversight**: Visualizes sponsor turns, dice rolls, team caps, and provides override controls. Admins can monitor fair turn order, shuffle teams, and ensure each sponsor selects once per round.
- **Match Admin Panel**: Offers scheduling overrides, live scorecard review, stats feeds, and Man of the Match overrides. Includes streaming controls and reel curation.
- **Ecommerce Management**: Seller approval queue, product upload console (stock, pricing, promotions), order fulfillment tracker, and payout/review reporting. Supports counter discount verification for app-downloaded customers.
- **Rewards & Analytics**: Configures lucky draw rules, monitors stacked spend, launches draws, and tracks prize fulfillment. Displays sponsor analytics (draft picks, player impact) and metrics for ecommerce/orders/reels engagement.
- **Content & Communication**: Assets for tournament posts (text + image), feeds for likes/comments, notification settings for all actors, and analytics dashboards covering reels, live streams, and standing updates.
